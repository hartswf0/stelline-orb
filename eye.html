THE EYE

The iris is every birthday video in the archive. The pupil is that birthday in three dimensions, measured from its own footage by tools/lift/lift.py. Nothing here is drawn by hand except generation 0.

RINGS

Iris: drag round to choose a birthday. Left arc: generation. Right arc: time through the clip (generation 3). Pupil: drag to move the eye round the cake; wheel or pinch to come closer.

GENERATIONS

G0 RIG: the earlier hand-made lathe and a flat colour.
G1 RELIEF: one frame pushed back by estimated depth. True from the front, hollow behind.
G2 REVOLVE: the cake measured as a solid of revolution with the footage projected onto it, candles standing on it, in the relief room. The room's depth scale is solved from the cake's own roundness.
G3 TIME: G2 measured at seven moments of the clip. The cake being made, in three dimensions.

SCORES

Each generation is drawn by this page and compared with the footage by tools/lift/score.mjs. SILHOUETTE: overlap with the cake in the frame, seen from the camera's own place. SOLID: the smallest outline the cake keeps as the eye goes half way round, against its outline from the front. COLOUR: how far the drawn cake is from the frame, 0 to 1. A generation passes when silhouette is at least 0.85 and solid at least 0.60.

NOT YET

The back of every cake is the mirror of its front. Hands, faces and smoke stay in the relief. A generated full mesh (image to 3D) is the next generation; its runner is tools/lift/fal3d.py and has not been run.
