# The Eye: the birthday archive in three dimensions

`eye.html` · `tools/lift/` · `assets/eye/` · run of 2026-11-11

The archive had 77 birthday videos and no third dimension. This pass measures each video's cake from its own
footage and shows every birthday through one instrument. Nothing here was generated by a paid service.

## What you can do

Open `eye.html` from a static server (`python3 -m http.server`, then `/eye.html`).

- **Iris**: all 77 birthdays as wedges. Drag round, or tap one, or use the left and right keys.
- **Pupil**: the chosen birthday in three dimensions. Drag to carry the eye round the cake; wheel or pinch to come closer.
- **Left arc**: the generation, G0 to G3. **Right arc**: time through the clip (G3).
- **FOOTAGE** puts the source video in the pupil. **ROOM** shows or hides the room. **TURN** orbits. **PLAY** runs
  the making of the cake. **GLB** saves the model in view.
- Links: `eye.html?id=bday_07&gen=2&yaw=40`.

## Generations

| | what it is | where it comes from |
|---|---|---|
| G0 rig | a lathe with one flat colour | the earlier pass (`profile_m` in `dataset.json`) |
| G1 relief | one frame pushed back by depth; exact from the camera, hollow behind | Depth Anything V2 (ViT-L), run locally |
| G2 revolve | the cake as a solid of revolution with the footage projected on it, candles standing on it, in the relief room | axis, pitch, profile and candles measured by `lift.py` |
| G3 time | G2 measured at seven moments of the clip: the cake being made | the same measurement, pitch held |
| G4 model | the cake built as geometry (`assets/eye/cakemodel.js`): icing body with ruffles or glaze, drips, piped shells and rosettes, beads, curls, neon lines, plate, candles with wax, wick and flame, lit with shadows | sizes from the measured profile, kinds from the clip's label (`cake_spec`), colours from the footage ring by ring, set to the label's icing colour |
| (later) | a generated full mesh per cake | **not run** (`tools/lift/fal3d.py`; fal answered "Exhausted balance") |

## Scores (all 77 clips, drawn by `eye.html`, measured by `tools/lift/score.mjs`)

| generation | pass | silhouette (mean) | solid (mean) | colour error (mean) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G0 rig | 3 / 77 | 0.684 | 1.000 | 0.215 |
| G1 relief | 13 / 77 | 0.908 | 0.519 | 0.002 |
| G2 revolve | 55 / 77 | 0.879 | 0.998 | 0.015 |
| G4 model | 37 / 77 | 0.838 | 0.995 | 0.261 |

- **silhouette**: overlap (intersection over union) of the drawn cake with the cake's mask in the frame, from the footage's own camera.
- **solid**: the smallest outline the cake keeps from 30 to 180 degrees round, over its outline from the front.
- **colour error**: mean absolute RGB difference between drawn cake and frame, 0 to 1.
- **pass**: silhouette at least 0.85 and solid at least 0.60. Both thresholds were set before the first full run and not changed.

Read these with their limits:

- G1 and G2 are the footage itself, pushed back or wrapped on a measured shape. They are not modeled cakes. G4 is the
  first generation that is: every part is a mesh. Its colour error is high because it is lit geometry, not the frame;
  its silhouette is lower than G2's because it adds a plate, borders and candles the mask does not always include.
- G4 takes its kind of icing, border and candles from the clip's label, so a wrong label makes a wrong cake. It has no
  writing, no roses, no figures or toppers yet, and the same seven part types serve all 77 cakes. All 77 on one sheet:
  `assets/eye/shots/g4-all-77.jpg`.

- The mask the silhouette is judged against comes from this pipeline's own segmenter, not from a person. The score
  shows that the solid agrees with the segmentation, not that the segmentation is right.
- The silhouette cannot detect a camera pitch that is too shallow. Pitch is therefore taken from the depth map where
  the fit is strong (48 clips), from the cake's edge ellipse (11), from the edge ellipse reduced until the solid fits
  its outline (16), or from the clip's shot family (2, not measured). Where both a strong depth fit and an edge read
  exist (43 clips) they differ by a median of 5.7 degrees; 38 of 43 are within 10.
- G3 has no score of its own: its last moment is G2, and its earlier moments have nothing independent to be judged against yet.
- The 22 G2 failures are listed in `assets/eye/scores.json`. Most sit between 0.80 and 0.85. The clear causes seen:
  cakes that are not round (`bday_47` a skyline, `bday_70` shards), a mask that caught only part of the cake
  (`bday_61`), and cakes cut by the edge of the picture.

## What is weak

- The back of every cake is the mirror of its front.
- Candles were found on 48 of 77 clips; unlit candles in front of the icing are often missed, and found candles can be short.
- Writing, roses and tiers that are not coaxial are only texture on a round solid.
- People, hands and smoke exist only in the relief room, which is true from the camera and fades out past 25 degrees.
- The depth scale of the room is solved from the cake on 58 clips; the other 19 use a default.
- No person has reviewed the renders, and no independent critic has been run. The builder's own look is the only check so far.
- The Eye's detent sounds are in the page; the phone layout was looked at once at 390 px wide.

## A finding about the earlier data

In the Weaver clips the world picture ends at row 433 of 720. `dataset.json` stores `y_split` 550, which includes
the top of the controller panel, so earlier measurements made "above y_split" included controller pixels. `lift.py`
finds the real row per clip (`world_bottom` in `lift.json`).

About 18 s per clip on 4 CPU cores for the lift, 3 minutes for scoring. `viz.py` draws the fits over the frames,
`plates.mjs` renders conditioning plates (eight camera families, depth pass, turntable), `film.mjs` records the Eye,
`review.mjs` makes sheets for a critic. `film.mjs` made `assets/eye/eye-proof.mp4`; `plates.mjs` and `review.mjs` are written and have not been run.

## A cake for someone (`cake-for.html`, `assets/eye/cakefor.js`)

The 77 archive cakes are bakery cakes with nobody in them. A real birthday cake starts from who it is for, so this
page starts there: age, name, message. Age picks the design (1 smash cake, 2 caterpillar, 3 ladybird, 4 train,
5 dinosaur, 6 castle, 7 rocket, 8 hedgehog, 9 to 12 and round birthdays the age as a number cake, otherwise a tall
drip cake); the design ring overrides it. One candle a year up to twelve, number candles after. The name is piped
as raised geometry from whatever is typed, in any script the browser has a font for.

Every design is built from the parts a home baker uses: sponge shapes, buttercream, fondant, sweets, biscuits,
ice-cream cones. They are original generic designs, not characters.

Not yet: these cakes are not in the Eye's iris and have no gate of their own (nothing in the archive to judge them
against); the designs are ten fixed sculptures, not a kit a child's request could recombine; faces are two eyes
and a nose; looked at only as rendered stills at twelve ages.

## Field Orb 06 (`orb-06.html`): Orb 05 plus WHO

A first attempt at Orb 06 was a new five-ring page written from nothing. It had no reticle and none of Orb 05's
operating theory, and it was removed. Orb 06 is now a fork of the Memory Weaver (`orb-film-studio.html`) and keeps
all of it: ring 1 MODE, ring 2 PICK, then verb, height on the cake, amount; the reticle beside the cake; the lab and
the memory as separate layers; frames in TIME; WEAVE; the guide whose SHOW ME plays each step on the rings.

What Orb 06 adds, inside that grammar:

| addition | how it is operated |
|---|---|
| WHO | `?name=NOOR&age=5`; the guide's header, steps and counts are written for that person |
| `WRITE · NAME` (red, white, chocolate) | a verb like any other: stand where the name should face, turn the amount ring; each click pipes the next stretch of single-stroke capitals onto the crown; turning back lifts it off. The letters are strokes, so they tilt and turn with the cake, as the film's lettering does |
| a candle for every year | the CANDLES step asks for exactly the person's age in clicks (twelve at most) |
| `EYES`, `BUTTONS`, `SWEETS`, `SPIKES` | new things for the PLACE verbs, set at the aimed height, one per click |
| `FROST · RED / GREEN / BLACK`, `PIPE · RED` | new colours for the existing verbs |
| designs by age | 1 to 2 SMASH, 3 to 5 LADYBIRD, 6 to 8 HEDGEHOG, 9 and up CLASSIC; `&design=` overrides |

Run and seen: the LADYBIRD guide for NOOR, 5, played by itself on the rings from the bare table through sponge,
red icing, black foot, ten button spots, two eyes, the name, five lit candles (stills
`assets/eye/shots/orb-06-stages.jpg`, film `assets/eye/orb-06-performance.mp4`, sped up four times because the
headless renderer is slow); the name TOMAS piped on a plain drum (`assets/eye/shots/orb-06-name.jpg`).

### Many pieces of clay, any memory

Orb 05 held one piece of clay. In Orb 06 a memory is any number of pieces (sixteen at most). One piece is in hand
and everything works on it exactly as before (verb, height, amount, reticle, piping, parts, the name); the others
stand where they were left. New verbs `PIECE · DRUM / DOME / TIERS / BALL / BLOCK / POST / CONE / BUNDT` set down
another piece (turning back takes it away). The PICK ring lists the other pieces, and touching one in the room takes
it in hand. MOVE, TURN and SIZE place the piece in hand. BALL, BLOCK, POST and CONE are plain building shapes, so
the pieces need not be a cake.

Built through the rings (the guide's own steps run at once by `FORGE.build()`, the same ring turns SHOW ME makes):
CATERPILLAR (four balls, eyes, a candle per segment), TRAIN (cab, boiler, funnel, two wagons with sweets, wheels and
candles), CASTLE (keep, four towers, four cone roofs, the name and six candles on the keep), and HORSE, the film's
carved horse on a plinth with `6 10 21` piped on it, which is not a cake. Stills: `assets/eye/shots/orb-06-pieces.jpg`.

Not checked for pieces: SHOW ME playing these guides at hand speed, picking a piece by touch, undo across a swap,
TIME frames and WEAVE with several pieces (pieces not in hand do not turn to mist), export of all pieces.

Not run: SMASH, HEDGEHOG and CLASSIC end to end; the steps after the candles (children, blow out, weave) under WHO;
a phone; sound. Known weak points: on the ladybird the eyes went on away from the camera and the name sits among the
spots; names are capitals A to Z and digits only, nine characters; the dinosaur and rocket of `cake-for.html` have no guide on the orb yet; number candles are not in the orb.
