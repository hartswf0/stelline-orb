## 2. Six Non-Negotiable SCION Invariants (Inherited Across All Future Work)

1. **Controller Lock & Screenless Museum Discipline:** Generative video models redraw complex UI panels over time. Either use the **Museum Screenless 5-Ring Barrel** (`?museum=1`, zero text, zero upper OLED) as a `UI LOCK` reference, OR generate **100% clean Stage-Only (`LAY-03`)** video and deterministically composite the live WebGL 5-ring barrel beneath it via `tools/flow/stack.sh`. When analyzing generated clips with CV, always detect and mask `y >= y_split` first.
2. **Reticle Purity (`)` Side Crescent Only):** Never wrap busy numbered lists, HUD callouts, or concentric target circles around the birthday cake. Either show **zero reticle (`reticle=off`)** or a **single pale smoked-glass `)` crescent standing to the right of the cake (`reticle=aside`)**, curving concave-toward-cake with subtle inward ticks.
3. **Orb-Free World Plates:** Inside the holographic memory room, **never** place an orb, camera, phone, or glowing gadget in any character's hands (only a cake knife, match, or plate). The Orb exists strictly as the operator's physical instrument.
4. **Independent Per-Layer Clocks (`Vesper Claims 1–18`):** Every addressable SAM/3D layer $i$ (`cake_body`, `icing_script`, `candle_flames`, `party_children`, `beetle_eyes`) owns an independent clock rate $r_i \in \{-1, 0, +1\}$. Holding `CHILDREN` at $r_{\text{kids}} = 0$ while jogging `CAKE` ($r_{\text{cake}} = 1$) must sweep live `3200K` candle bounce across their frozen faces.
5. **Transient Mechanical Foley Only (Zero Synth Drones):** Never instantiate continuous `OscillatorNode` drones or ambient hums. Emit only `3ms–5ms` bandpass-filtered noise impulses (`1100Hz–3100Hz`, rate-limited `>55ms`) on physical ball-detent crossings and switch actuations.
6. **Blind Dual-Critic Verification:** Every new artifact or model run must pass **Critic A** (Blind Visual/Temporal Frame Critic: `16:9` aspect, grounded table contact shadow, reticle restraint, lighting falloff on blow-out) and **Critic B** (Bijective Operative Surface & Practical Utility Critic: every visual delta caused by a real ring/wing gesture, zero-fault CAD/export utility).

## 2b. FUTURE II, Made Real: The Recipe Book On Field Orb 05 (`RECIPE` mode · `recipes.html`)

Prompt C asked for a compiler from craft video to *"the shortest human-legible sequence of physical controller operations"*. It now exists inside the instrument itself, not as a separate replay page.

**What it is.** `field-orb-05.html` has a tenth mode on ring 1, `RECIPE`. Ring 2 chooses a shelf (`FILM · 6`, `ARCHETYPE · 41`, `CLIP · 77`), ring 3 a reference plate, ring 4 the pace, ring 5 builds it. A recipe is **not a stored model**. It is the plate's `cake_spec` (from `BDAY TRAINING/labels.json`) compiled, at the moment you ask, into the orb's own ring clicks, and then performed on the rings where you can watch the hand. Touch any ring or key while it builds and the book lets go; the cake stays, yours to finish with the same rings.

**The bijection, stated as a table** (`BOOK.compile` in `field-orb-05.html`):

| Plate says (`cake_spec`) | The rings do |
| :--- | :--- |
| `tiers` 1 / 2 / 3, `flutes` | `MAKE` · VERB `BASE · DRUM / TIERS / 3 TIERS / BUNDT` · ring 5 once |
| `flutes` 6–16 | VERB `FLUTE · 12` · HEIGHT up the ring in five steps · two clicks each |
| `finish: faceted`, `form: hex` | VERB `FACET · 6` · four clicks at alternate heights |
| `icing_start`, `icing_end` | Loaded as `PLATE A` / `PLATE B`. `FROST · PLATE A` swept foot to crown, then `FROST · PLATE B` over it: the colour turns as it does in the clip |
| `finish: ruffle` | VERB `RUFFLE` · a thin band pulled at each height |
| `drips`, `finish: neon_circuit` | `PIPE · CHOCOLATE` / `PIPE · NEON` round the shoulder or the side |
| `border: beads / shells / neon_ring` | `PIPE · WHITE` closed at the crown edge / `ROSETTES` in the last bag's colour / `PIPE · NEON` at foot and crown |
| `inscribe` (+ `inscribe_style`) | VERB `WRITE · RED / GOLD / NEON` · ring 5 until the words are whole. The text is cut into rows and laid where a ray from above meets the clay, so it follows the sculpted crown. `&say=` writes your own |
| `topping` | `BERRIES` at the foot (and the ledge), `ROSETTES`, or `SPRINKLES` at four heights |
| `candles`, `flame` | `CANDLES` · one click each (the ring closes up evenly past seven) · `BLOW OUT` · and for `rewinding`, ring 5 turned back so the flames return |
| archetype height, `tilt_deg` | `SIZE` · HEIGHT · and `MOVE` up, `TURN` pitch |
| `stage_cast` | VERB `A CHILD` · three clicks |
| `camera_rig_preset` | `LOOK` · ORBIT, TILT, DOLLY to the clip's own lens |

**Deep links.** `field-orb-05.html?build=<id>` where `<id>` is a film recipe (`film_canon`, `film_bundt`, `film_drum`, `film_dome`, `film_tiers`, `film_wish`), an archetype id (`canonical_chamber_7` …) or a clip (`bday_01` … `bday_78`). `&pace=1|2|4|12`, `&say=<your words>`.

**What was filmed and cut.** `tools/recipes/record_recipes.cjs` steps the instrument frame by frame and films each build (`assets/recipes/<id>.mp4` + a `.json` of step times). `tools/recipes/cut_every_cake.py` cuts **`assets/recipes/every-cake-built.mp4`** to the beat of `mix1_tizita_stelline_soul.mp3` (73.8 bpm, measured): plates are cropped above `y_split × 0.78` so no generated controller is ever shown (Invariant 1), blow-outs are cut where each clip measurably loses its light, and the last shot is every filmed build at once. `tools/recipes/build_recipe_index.py` indexes the films for `recipes.html`.

**The second edit: blending and multiplying (`assets/recipes/every-cake-multiplied.mp4`, `tools/recipes/cut_multiplied.py`).** Eight techniques, each a function over frames: `slits` (one film moment as plate / segmentation / depth+edge / coded 3D in sliding bands; the first three are registered, so a face continues across the cut), `cutouts` (film sprite sheets standing in the coded room), `swap` (a cast held, the cake changed each half beat), `parade` (the 77 isolated cakes), `blend` (synthetic plate × coded build: slats, wipe, strobe, lighten), `grid` (the same breath 1 → 4 → 9 → 20, each tile started so its own measured loss of light lands on one beat; the flames go within about half a second of each other), `rewind`, and `stack`. **How many of the same moment are shown at once:** 20 in a grid (every blow-out in the corpus; at 5 × 4 a tile is 254 px wide and faces still read) and 3 in a lighten stack (kept to three because the faces already overlap). Neither ceiling was pushed further and tested. `record_recipes.cjs --hero 4` films the finished cake circled by the camera with no controller in frame, for the coded side of the blends.

**Honest limits (epistemic ledger).** `OBSERVED`: the plates and their measured colours, candle counts and cut times. `INFERRED`: the mapping from a plate's description to ring gestures (the table above). The builds match each plate's *description*, not its pixels: a ruffle is five pulled bands, not that clip's exact ruffle; lettering is laid in piped rows, not traced from the frame; candle styles (`neon`, `crystal`, `sparkler`) and era casts are not yet distinguished (the three chamber children stand in for every era). The next step for Future II is closing that gap from `segmentation.profile_m` (the measured silhouette is already in the labels).

## 2c. One Grid, One Bar: Normalizing The Corpus Toward Orb 05 (`assets/normalized/`, `tools/normalize/`)

The question was how to bring every clip, and every model made from a clip, onto the same footing. The answer built here has three parts, each a file you can run.

1. **One grid** (`tools/normalize/register.py`). The Mask R-CNN / GrabCut masks survive only as the tinted middle panel of `assets/bday-training/seg/<id>_seg.webp`. They are read back (panel 2 minus 0.45 × panel 1 leaves the tint), the cake's box is taken, and a zoom and shift are solved that put the cake at centre x = 640, crown at y = 250, 480 px wide in the 1280 × 576 picture. Plates are zoomed, never shrunk, and shifted only as far as the zoom allows, so no frame edge is ever bared.
2. **One bar.** Each clip has one event: the breath (the half second in which it loses most light), the turn (`morph_jumps_s`), or its arrival. A bar is 4 beats at 73.83 bpm (78 frames) and the event sits on the first frame of beat 3. Clips are offset, not time-warped, except where a clip is too short on one side of its event: those are slowed just enough, never below half speed.
3. **One instrument.** `record_recipes.cjs --twin` has the Memory Weaver build the same clip's recipe, then films it for the same bar with the camera solved (by projecting the cake's rim) to put the 3D cake on the plates' grid, and blows it out with ring 5 from the first frame of beat 3. The orb gained a verb for this, `THE PARTY`: the cast of the plate's own era, cut from the plates by segmentation, stood behind the table as one cut-out (`ERAS` in `field-orb-05.html`; a second click walks on to the next era). So a twin is a 3D cake, a segmented cast and a locked lens, all in Orb 05.

**The cull is stated, not implied.** `assets/normalized/index.json` holds, for all 77 clips, the cake box, the fit (zoom, shift, how far off the grid it still is), the event, and `keep` with `reasons`. 33 are kept. 24 are dropped for generator caption text in the picture, the other 20 because the cake cannot be brought to the grid (too large to fit without shrinking, or too far off centre). The caption detector (thin bright glyphs on dark, in the two zones the generator writes in) was calibrated against 71 clips judged by eye and is overridden by that judgement where they disagree; both lists are in the file.

**What it buys the edit** (`tools/normalize/cut_one_grid.py`): any frame of any bar can follow any frame of any other and the cake does not move; anything shown side by side is already in time. Four cuts use only that: `one-grid.mp4`, `nine-rooms.mp4` (one cake of two halves, plate left of a moving seam and coded twin right), `the-breath.mp4`, `the-turn.mp4`. `vision/<id>.png` is what the segmentation saw, carried through the same transform.

**What it does not do yet.** The registration is one box from one frame per clip, so a clip whose camera moves drifts off the grid within its bar. The twin matches the plate's position, size and beat, not its lens: every twin is filmed at one pitch. The masks are Mask R-CNN and GrabCut, not Segment Anything; rerunning segmentation per frame with SAM 2 would give a moving box, a per-frame cast cut-out for the orb instead of one still sheet per era, and a silhouette to fit the lathe profile to.

## 2d. Standard Cuts, Looks, The Best Threes, And Orb 06

**A cut is a file** (`stelline-timeline/1`, `tools/timeline/assemble.py`). Shots are given in beats; a shot's place is the sum of the beats before it, so shots reorder without retiming. Each shot names an `op` (`bar`, `seen`, `seam`, `triptych`, `tiles`, `flicker`, `card`), a `clip` from `assets/normalized/index.json`, a `layer` (`plate` or `twin`), `at` (where in the clip's own four-beat bar it starts) and optionally a `segment` (`cake` or `people`: only what that mask covers is shown). The five cuts in `assets/normalized/timelines/` are written by `cut_one_grid.py` and rendered by the one assembler: `python3 tools/timeline/assemble.py <file> [--look …]`.

**Two looks** (`tools/timeline/looks.py`), applied to the picture only: `panavision70` (lifted warm blacks, amber highlights short of white, red halation round flames, horizontal softness, grain, corner fall-off) and `camcorder90` (half resolution, chroma smeared and shifted, sharpening halos, raised saturation, scan lines, chroma noise). They are imitations from general knowledge of the two media, not measured from a print or a tape. Their use is practical: under one grade a synthetic plate and its coded twin read as one stock (`nine-rooms.mp4`).

**The best threes** (`tools/normalize/triads.py`, `assets/normalized/triads.json`). Shot types are the corpus's own angle families, plus the event each bar carries. Each kept bar is measured for colour (lightness, chroma, dominant hue, a chroma-weighted hue histogram) and composition (how near the grid the cake sits, how evenly people fall either side of it, headroom). Every set of three within a type is scored two ways, both multiplied by the three composition scores: `MATCH` (shared palette) and `CONTRAST` (hues as far apart as possible at similar lightness). Eight types have three or more kept clips. The scores rank sets against each other within a type; they are not a judgement of taste, and the weights are mine.

**Orb 06, the Archive Orb** (`field-orb-06.html`). Five rings: `SHELF` (all kept, each shot type, each era, and the dropped clips with the reason each was dropped), `PLATE`, `VIEW` (plate, twin, seam, seen, cake only, people only, three, three apart), `LOOK`, `BEAT` (quarter beats through the bar). Keys: `PLAY` loops the bar, `LEN` sets shot length, `ADD` puts what is showing on the cut, `UNDO`, `PLAY CUT`, `SAVE` writes a `stelline-timeline/1` file. `?timeline=<url>` opens a written cut. Limits: the looks in the orb are previews (canvas filters), the assembler applies the real grade; `tiles`, `flicker` and `card` shots in an opened cut are shown as a label, not played; it operates the 33 normalized clips and their twins and browses the 44 dropped ones, not the other 139 videos in the repository.

## 2e. Full Build-Outs: Each Scene With Its Own Cake, Its Own People, On One 16-Beat Clock

**How many real 3D builds are filmed, and how.** Three kinds exist, and only the last is a whole scene:

| Films | Count | What is filmed | On the grid | People |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Recipe films (`assets/recipes/<id>.mp4`) | 47 | The build, with the orb in frame | No | The three chamber children |
| Coded twins (`assets/normalized/bars/<id>_twin.mp4`) | 33 | The finished cake for one bar | Yes | One still sheet per era |
| **Full build-outs** (`assets/normalized/builds/<id>.mp4`) | 33 | The build from the empty table, then the bar | Yes | **That clip's own, moving** (22 clips; 11 plates have no people) |

**Its own people** (`tools/normalize/cast.py`). For each kept clip, eight frames spread over its bar are multiplied by its people mask and cropped: a sprite strip of that clip's faces and hands, with a gap where its cake was. The Memory Weaver loads it with the recipe (`setPlateCast`, `KIDS[14]`); `THE PARTY` then brings that cast, not the era sheet. In a build-out the strip follows the bar's clock, so they breathe out as the candles go out.

**Put back where they stood** (`record_recipes.cjs --buildout`). The recipe is built once unseen to solve the grid camera, then built again from nothing and filmed through that locked camera. The cast sheet is sized and placed from the box it had in the plate, corrected for standing 0.44 m behind the cake.

**One clock** (`tools/normalize/buildouts.py`). The making is retimed in two straight segments so the first candle lands on beat 9 (the downbeat of bar 3); the last four beats are the bar as filmed, breath on beat 15. `assets/normalized/builds/index.json` records each scene's steps and where its people arrive.

**The standard, and its audit** (`tools/normalize/audit.py`, `assets/normalized/standard.json`). An early pass filmed the build-outs as a clean stage with no controller and no reticle. That is no longer accepted. A clip is up to standard only when all eight of these are measured true: `BAR` (78 frames, event on frame 39), `TWIN` (78 frames), `BUILD-OUT` (exactly 312 frames), `CONTROLLER` (the real orb filmed in the same pass, frame for frame as long as the build-out), `RETICLE` (the reference reticle: the programmed `)` crescent carried over from `orb-film-studio.html` into Orb 05, measured from the cake's bounds every frame so it stands to its right, no text; it was present in the SVG layer on the last filmed frame), `GRID` (the 3D cake within 12 px of centre, 16 px of width and 14 px of crown), `CLOCK` (first candle on beat 9), `PEOPLE` (the clip's own cast wherever the plate has people). The frame for anything the orb made is the stack (SCION Invariant 1): the picture above, the controller beneath it, never over it.

**Four versions, one material** (`assets/normalized/timelines/`): `build-outs` (each era as plate, making, people, breath); `from-the-film` (the film's own two shots, the room before the cake and the cake appearing, then *the making starts* and everything after is made by the rings); `video-to-game` (seven clips honed in eight one-beat steps from video to a scene the orb plays); `reverse-inception` (the same bar nested at three depths, made scene ⊃ twin ⊃ video, then turned inside out). Two shot types were added for them: `film` (a stretch of the source film) and `nest`.

**Limits.** The cast is a flat sheet cut with one mask for all eight poses, so anyone who moves outside the mask is clipped, and the sheet does not parallax. Where the plate's own candles fall inside the people mask they remain on the sheet, behind the orb's candles (cutting them out left candle-shaped holes). The cake is the recipe's reading of the plate, not its pixels (candle styles and decoration differ). The table and room are Orb 05's, the same in every scene. The 44 dropped clips have no build-out.

## 2f. Two Outside Loops Run Against The Archive: A Game (`games/breath/`)

The request was to run `lovisdotio/video-to-game` and Video2Game to make real games. What happened, plainly:

- **video-to-game** (an agent skill, MIT, cloned and read): its loop was followed step for step **except the one it rests on**, generating every asset on fal. Every call to `queue.fal.run` returned `403 User is locked. Reason: Exhausted balance`. The calls are written (`tools/games/fal_assets.py`) and unrun. The game therefore uses assets this repository had already taken from the videos.
- **Video2Game** (repository cloned and read): it trains a NeRF from many posed views on a CUDA GPU. The videos were never the missing piece: only 3 of the 77 clips orbit, generated clips do not hold one object still between frames, and this machine has no GPU. So it was not trained. Instead the orb's scene of clip 35 was captured as the dataset Video2Game reads (72 views, exact poses, cake masks, COLMAP binary), checked with Video2Game's own reader (99.7% of cake points land inside the mask in every view), and its commands were pointed at it: `datasets/v2g/`, `tools/games/v2g/`. In the game its shape is kept: geometry (the orb's cake as GLB), a collider, simple physics, a browser.

**The game, The Breath.** You are the breath. Aim with mouse or arrows, hold space to blow, carry it from flame to flame before the candles burn down; a breath begun on the third beat carries further; the next cake comes with its own people; five cakes make a sitting. Each of the 33 kept clips is a level: its cake as a real 3D model (`levels/<id>/cake.glb`, built by Orb 05's rings and exported by `tools/games/export_levels.cjs`), its own cast, the archive's grid camera and bar clock. The lung, the beat and the candles' time are read off the `)` crescent; there is no other interface. `games/breath/README.md` tabulates each step of both loops against what was done, what stood in, and what differs from the videos.

**Proved by play, as that skill requires** (`tools/games/play.cjs`, `pairs.cjs`): a keyboard-only scenario reading only `window.__game.state` plays and films a sitting; two bad tunings were found and fixed that way; side-by-side pairs with the plates exposed a lighting fault, since fixed.
