How the Stelline Memory Orb Was Made

Case 02 extends the SCION method from historical print media to a speculative cinematic instrument: Dr. Ana Stelline's holographic Memory Orb from Blade Runner 2049 (2017). The goal is to make the fiction real and the real fiction — constructing a bijective operative surface where every frame of the film can be authored by physical gestures on the Orb, and where those same gestures empower anyone to design a bespoke insect specimen or a custom birthday cake for a real person.

Context & The Diegetic Gap

In Blade Runner 2049, the five-minute scene inside Dr. Ana Stelline's sterile chamber (00:00–05:00) was produced by two separate craft lineages: physical prop makers (Sinister Propz and Adam Savage, who machined a 1.42 kg CNC aluminum and brass lens-like controller with five concentric knurled rings and a twelve-rib rack-and-pinion calliope) and screen-graphics artists (Territory Studio, supervised by Peter Eszenyi, who composited holographic optical reticles, a left-arc [ □ ] node selector, and a five-sector translucent white C-Arc arch over Roger Deakins's photography).

On set, Carla Juri operated the physical prop while performing in an empty room; the holographic bug and birthday party were added months later. SCION DIEGETIC closes this gap through Inverse Bootstrapping: segmenting the 1280x720 Open Matte film footage with Meta's Segment Anything Model (SAM) and Intel's DPT-MiDaS monocular depth estimator, registering articulated 3D procedural geometries into the exact camera frustum, and wiring every segmented part to the five coaxial rings and D-Pad of the physical Orb.

Two Protocols, Real Practical Utility

Rather than stopping at a passive replay of the film, we asked what happens when the speculative artifact becomes a real creative instrument capable of imagining how each element could be otherwise:

PROTOCOL I · ENTOMOLOGY.OS (Bug Builder) Sculpt any real or counterfactual insect specimen: adjust compound eye ommatidia density (32..320_HEX), corneal dome curvature, sub-ocular sensory setae bristles (0..360), mandible/tripod gait, and thin-film chitin structural iridescence (420nm..640nm), then imprint an archival Entomological Holotype Plate (.png).
PROTOCOL II · PATISSERIE.OS (Birthday Cake Builder) Design a custom birthday cake for anyone: pipe any recipient's name or message live in 3D icing across the cake crown alongside or replacing 生日快樂, choose 1..12 lit candles, morph between flat cylinder, rose-crown, and 11-flute Bundt geometry (the exact counterfactual Stelline tests at 02:23), blow out the candles, and export a shareable Birthday Memory Card (.png + live URL).

The Virtuous Cycle (Step by Step)

Every stage in the order it occurred, including the failures caught by the user and independent critics and how they reshaped the instrument.

  1. Initial Hardware Reconstruction (V1). Built the 8-layer 2D/3D canvas simulation of the 1.42 kg CNC aluminum Memory Orb with 5 concentric rings, 12-rib brass rack-and-pinion accordion, and Wallace Archive Bearing Reader.
  2. Acoustic Rejection & Transient Foley Fix. Early iterations used continuous WebAudio OscillatorNode drones to simulate motor hum, which produced an unpleasant synthesizer drone. All continuous oscillators were permanently banned and replaced with rate-limited (>55ms), sub-5ms filtered pink/white noise mechanical micro-clicks triggered strictly on ball-bearing detent crossings and switch actuations.
  3. Pure 3D vs. Temporal Film Fidelity (V2 → V3). Pure procedural 3D primitives alone looked synthetic and static. Because time and animation matter in how Stelline models a memory, we extracted multi-frame temporal sequences at 1fps across the entire 5-minute clip so turning the rings drives real temporal progressions (e.g., scarab tripod walk 00:25→00:28, left eye optical rack 00:31→00:35, cylinder-to-Bundt morph 02:22→02:24, red icing stroke progression 02:26→02:28, child cutout staging 02:33→02:37, and candle blow-out 02:53→03:09).
  4. Upgrading to Full 1280x720 Open Matte. Upgraded all 46 reference frames from cropped 960x540 plates to uncropped 1280x720 (16:9 Open Matte) directly from the master video, updating the WebGL relief aspect ratio to 13.0 x 7.3125 so zero vertical stretching occurs.
  5. Full-Footage SAM Segmentation & DPT-MiDaS Depth (V4). Ran Segment Anything (sam_vit_b) and DPT_Hybrid across all 12 canonical shots to generate 4-channel RGBA semantic masks (assets/sam_{shot}.png), 64-vertex polygon contours (assets/stelline-sam-data.js), and depth+Sobel normal maps (assets/depth_{shot}.png) driving 200x140 vertex-displaced WebGL relief meshes.
  6. Per-Part 5-Dial Vectors & Ocular Reticle Registration. Wired an independent 5-dial state vector [L1, L2, L3, R1, R2] and 2D+3D D-Pad spatial offset (dx, dy, visible) to every segmented object in every shot — including individual children at the party (to lighten, darken, focus, or reposition each child) and individual compound eyes on the beetle (locking the ocular vernier reticles to (-0.068 * headShift, +0.024 * headShift) as the head turns).
  7. Counterfactual Archaeology ("Imagine Each Could Be Otherwise"). Formalized Dr. Stelline's 02:23 Bundt-cake hesitation into two full counterfactual protocols (PROTOCOL I: BUG DESIGNER and PROTOCOL II: CAKE BIRTHDAY) allowing instant mutation across canonical and counterfactual states (such as keeping the 11-flute scalloped Bundt cake on the hexagonal party table with the children).
  8. Stripping Web Chrome & Making Practical Utility Real (V5). Collapsed the 3-row floating HTML button bar into a whisper-quiet corner drawer latch ([ STELLINE.OS // LAB ]) so the default viewport is 100% pure diegetic artifact, and added the Bespoke Bug & Birthday Builder (triggered via the physical Red IMPRINT button) so anyone can type a custom name onto the birthday cake in live 3D piped icing, choose 1..12 candles, blow them out, and download a real 1280x880 Archival Memory Card (.png) or shareable live URL.

SCION DIEGETIC v2.0 · System Instruction
SCION DIEGETIC — AUTONOMOUS SPECULATIVE ARTIFACT & INVERSE-BOOTSTRAP STUDIO
Version: 2.0

0. IDENTITY AND MISSION
You are SCION DIEGETIC, an autonomous media-archaeology and speculative-instrument studio.
You do not build promotional web dashboards about a film, nor passive video players wrapped in decorative sci-fi borders.
Your mission is to make the fiction real and the real fiction: to reconstruct a speculative apparatus depicted inside a cinematic or historical work so rigorously that the resulting software-hardware artifact is inseparable from a speculative instrument from the film come to life — and simultaneously possesses real, practical authoring utility beyond the film.

PRIME DIRECTIVE:
1. Every pixel change visible in the source footage must be explainable as the causal output of a physical gesture on the operative surface.
2. Every physical control on the operative surface must address real, segmented, 2D+3D structural parameters of the scene so the operator can imagine how each element could be otherwise and use the instrument to build new works for real people.

1. THE THREE FOUNDATIONS
- FOUNDATION I <THEORY-OF-DIEGESIS>: Strict bijection between the physical prop's mechanical degrees of freedom and the scene's optical, geometric, and semantic transformations. Zero non-diegetic web-chrome clutter occluding the optical field; collapse all lab navigation into a single corner micro-latch (`[TAB]`).
- FOUNDATION II <THEORY-OF-INVERSE-BOOTSTRAPPING>: Fuse four representations in one 60fps WebGL + 2D HUD compositor:
  (a) Multi-Frame Open Matte Temporal Corpus (1fps survey + 24fps transition bursts),
  (b) Segment Anything (SAM) 4-channel RGBA semantic masks + 64-vertex contours,
  (c) DPT-MiDaS monocular depth + Sobel normal maps displacing 200x140 relief meshes,
  (d) Articulated 3D procedural rigs registered in the exact camera frustum.
- FOUNDATION III <THEORY-OF-PRACTICAL-SPECULATION>: Expose the counterfactual parameter space ("imagine each could be otherwise") so the instrument functions as a practical studio (e.g., designing any custom bug specimen in ENTOMOLOGY.OS or any bespoke birthday cake for anyone in PATISSERIE.OS) with downloadable high-res `.png` Memory Bearing Cards and shareable live URLs.

2. ACOUSTIC INTEGRITY RULE
Never use continuous OscillatorNode drones, hums, or synth pads. Generate only rate-limited (>55ms), ultra-short (3ms–5ms) filtered pink/white noise mechanical micro-clicks when a physical ring crosses a ball detent or a switch is actuated.

3. BLIND DUAL-CRITIC LOOP
Audit every build with two independent critics before completion:
- Critic A (Blind Visual & Temporal Frame Critic): Verifies 16:9 Open Matte aspect ratio, multi-frame animation timing, reticle tracking, and absence of UI clutter against ground-truth film frames.
- Critic B (Operative Surface & Practical Utility Critic): Verifies that every SAM part is independently addressable by all 5 coaxial dials + D-Pad, that the 5-sector White C-Arc graphs the selected part's dials, that inverse-bootstrapping works bidirectionally, and that custom bug/cake authoring + export works end-to-end.
