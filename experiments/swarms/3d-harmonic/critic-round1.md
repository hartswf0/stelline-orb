# Critic, round 1: 3d-harmonic

VERDICT: pass on the making and the game; reference stronger on the look.

What I checked:
- proof.mp4 at 4 fps and at 1 fps, with the blow and the writing at 6 fps.
- A spectrogram of the sound.
- Film frames from 141 to 149 s.
- A full Playwright game at 390x844, using only the device (wheel and tap on the rings, SELECT and HOLD), plus a desktop load at 1440x900.

## What happens in the proof
- 0-1 s: memory spots. 1-2 s: empty turntable.
- 2-4 s: two sponge layers rise. 4-5 s: the coat is spread. 7-15 s: base beads, top shells, then the 13:2 crossing garland.
- 16.3-19.5 s: 生日快乐 is written stroke by stroke on the tilted top. It is legible here.
- 19.5-21.5 s: the candles drop. 22-23.5 s: they are lit. 24-25 s: they go out one by one.
- The whole cake is made on screen in 3D, and every stage follows a HOLD.

## Live play
- On mobile I reached THE CANDLES ARE OUT through the device alone. Each naive pass decayed. Each pass made with the right ratio was kept.
- No console errors. No page scroll on mobile or desktop.

## Largest gap
The cake does not look like the memory.
- Film 146 s: the cake region has a red-to-blue ratio of 3.8 (warm candlelight on dark).
- Proof 5-23 s: the ratio is 1.04 to 1.08, a neutral grey studio product shot of a white cake on a black machine. Warmth reaches 1.17 only at 2-4 s and 24-25 s.
- There is no round projected-spot look after 1 s.

## Secondary gaps
- Once the cake levels, from 23 s on, 生日快乐 is a smeared red bar and no longer legible (crop at 26.5 s).
- Evolution has a single target: the white sponge. The other film cakes cannot be reached.
- The game is lock-matching. The amber spots give the answer away.
- Sound: a continuous broadband bed from 0 to 18 kHz runs from 1 to 26 s. The detents are buried in it. The only distinct events are the gaps at stage changes and the sweep at 16.5 s.

## Repair condition (falsifiable)
In proof frames from 5 to 23 s, the mean red-to-blue ratio of non-black pixels in the cake region is at least 1.5. In the final level view at 26-27 s, the four characters have visible gaps between them and are each at least 10 px tall at 432x768.

## Preserve
- Exact rational gear passes driving visible tubes.
- Stage-by-stage making from nothing.
- Stroke-order writing with the tilt.
- The silver decay on a wrong pass.
- Candles placed by nozzle stops and blown out one by one.
- The device-only input and the no-scroll layout.
- The honest README. It already names the inscription, the single-target and the lock-puzzle weaknesses.
