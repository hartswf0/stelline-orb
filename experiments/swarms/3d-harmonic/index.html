HARMONIC CAKE · THE OPERATOR PANEL

A cake decorated by a gear train. The turntable turns the cake; the piping arm and the nozzle move on gear-driven harmonic paths; every path is a closed figure set by whole-number gear ratios and a phase. Nothing is keyframed. The goal is the film's sponge cake with white beads: a bead ring at the foot, shells round the top, a crossing garland on the side, 生日快乐 written stroke by stroke, seven candles, lit, then blown out.

SELECTPicks the gear you are setting: TURNTABLE, PIPING ARM, NOZZLE.
KNURLED RINGThe selected gear's ratio to the master shaft, in rational detents (1:4 ... 1:1, 5:4, 4:3, 3:2, 5:3, 7:4, 2:1 ... 36:1). Turn by hand; the machine then steps to it at 11.8 a second, one click a step (23.6 when more than six behind).
TRIANGLE RINGThe selected gear's phase, in 15 degree detents.
SCALE RINGWhere you kneel: your angle around the cake.
HOLDRuns one pass of the machine with the gears as they stand. A pass that lands within 0.8 mm (mean) of the pattern stays; one that does not decays away and the stage stays. Hold it down to switch the memory off.
ON THE CAKERound amber spots: the pattern the memory wants. Thin pale line: where your gears would pipe. The scope beside the arc shows both unrolled, with the distance.
LEFT COLLARThe play button plays the game by itself through the same rings; the bar clears it.
