SCULPTED BIRTHDAY · OPERATOR

The cake is one mesh of 1298 points, worked by hand like dough. Each handle (TOP CENTRE, TIER RING, RIM, SIDE BAND, BASE) is a ring of points you push or pull; everything between is solved as-rigid-as-possible (Sorkine and Alexa 2007): every point gets its own best rotation, then all positions are solved together, and the two steps repeat. HOLD bakes the shape (the dough sets) and, once the shape is close enough to one of the film's cakes, starts the next layer. Icing, piped beads, the writing and the candles ride the surface; the beads and candles turn with each point's rotation, so they stay their own shape.

The small picture is the same moves solved the naive way: one linear solve, no rotations. Its beads and letters shear when the surface turns.

  • SELECT: which handle, or the layer being made.
  • KNURLED RING: push or pull the handle along its normal; or how far the layer has gone on.
  • TRIANGLE RING: slide the handle along the surface (TOP CENTRE and BASE: twist); for a layer, tilt the cake toward you.
  • SCALE RING: where you kneel.
  • HOLD: bake and go on. Long press: switch the memory off. Left collar: play the scene, or clear.
