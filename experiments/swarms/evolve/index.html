GROWN CAKE · OPERATOR

The cake is made in eight stages. The first five are grown: each generation the machine breeds eight variants around the current cake and keeps the one closest to the reference, a frame of the film (2:35.0, her birthday cake at the table) reduced to a traced outline and colour histograms. The frame is not shipped. Then the inscription is written stroke by stroke, the seven candles are placed and lit, and blown out.

HOLD (short): breed one generation, or set the stage when its bar is reached, light, blow. HOLD (long): switch the memory off. SELECT: which made stage to work. KNURLED RING: mutation strength; in the inscription, the strokes; in the candles, how many. TRIANGLE RING: lean the mutation toward one trait. SCALE RING: where you kneel.
