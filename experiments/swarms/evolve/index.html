GROWN BIRTHDAY · OPERATOR

The head and the cake are not drawn. Each is a genome of numbers; each generation eight variants are bred around the current form and the closest to the reference is kept. The references are two frames of the film (3:34.0, the birthday girl; 2:35.0, her cake), reduced to traced outlines, five landmarks and a colour histogram. The frames are not shipped.

HOLD (short): breed one generation. HOLD (long): switch the memory off. SELECT: head or cake, and which trait group mutates. KNURLED RING: mutation strength. TRIANGLE RING: lean the mutation toward one trait. SCALE RING: where you kneel.
