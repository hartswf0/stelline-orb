FOUR PAIRS OF HANDS · HOW TO PLAY

Four children make the birthday cake on the table, from an empty table to seven lit candles, and the birthday child blows them out. Every part of the cake is put there by a child's hands. The children have no faces; you read them by height, clothes and what their arms are doing.

SELECTPicks a child: 1 the birthday child (the board, then the blow), 2 the layers, 3 icing, beads and the writing, 4 the candles and the light.
KNURLED RINGAdvances the selected child's next making action, one detent at a time. Your hand turn clicks quietly; then the machine steps to it at 11.8 a second. A child whose next action is not yet possible will not move; the ring stops and the readout says what is missing.
TRIANGLE RINGThe selected child's reach, -6 to +6. It changes how high and how far she stretches, and it decides what she puts on the cake: the sponge (low chocolate, middle plain, high red), the icing colour (five steps from orange to brown), the beads (low white, high coloured). Each finished action is a new generation of the cake; the line at the top names the film cake it is now nearest to.
SCALE RINGWhere you kneel around the table, in degrees.
HOLDAll the children perform together: the machine runs the remaining actions in order, with each child's current reach. Press again to stop. Hold it down to switch the memory off.
LEFT COLLARThe play mark plays the whole making through the same rings; the bar clears the table.

Technique: each child is one three.js SkinnedMesh with 23 bones, vertex weights computed in rig.js, a forearm split over two twist bones, and six corrective shapes (elbows, shoulder raise, shoulder forward) baked from a dual-quaternion reference. The printed badge on child 2's left sleeve and the bow on child 1's dress are region-masked so they move as one piece. Measurements: operator page. Side-by-side stress test: stress view.
