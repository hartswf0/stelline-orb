CONSTRAINT BIRTHDAY · OPERATOR PANEL

Light all seven candles with the taper she holds, then press HOLD and she blows them out. Her feet stay planted, her limbs keep their lengths, her elbow bends one way, and she cannot fall over. You never place her body: you move the point the taper's flame is held by (the light pen) and the cake, and the solver moves everything else.

SELECT picks HAND or CAKE. HAND: the knurled ring moves the pen along the table, the triangle ring moves it up and down. CAKE: the knurled ring slides the cake along the table (it stays on the top), the triangle ring turns it, which carries every candle round with it. A candle can be lit only when turning has brought it into her plane (the dashed line across the cake). The scale ring turns your view. Each detent you pass is a quiet click; the machine then steps to it at 11.8 steps a second, re-solving her body after every step. HOLD long-press switches the memory off.
