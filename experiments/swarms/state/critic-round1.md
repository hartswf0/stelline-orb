# Critic, round 1: state / Her Birthday

**Verdict: pass, with one real gap.**

**Evidence**
- Calling her makes her refuse. proof.mp4 0.0–1.0 s: she faces the camera. 1.25–4.0 s: she turns her back and holds it while the device reads CALLING HER 3 OF 6. There are four call tones in the spectrogram at 1.8, 3.9, 5.1 and 7.3 s. In my phone playtest, six calls held her in the turned-back sulk for the whole 7 s, and nothing she did read as obedience.
- Pushing the cake makes her hide. With CAKE TOWARD HER at 112 cm she sulked for about 3 s, then dropped into a crouch (phone, 25.8–28 s). Facing away and crouching are easy to tell apart at 390 px.
- She can be earned. proof 14–17 s: she walks to the table. 18.0–19.0 s: the flames go out and smoke rises, and the spectrogram has broadband breath bursts at 17.3–19.3 s that line up. As a naive player (light down, cake lowered and near, three children, kneeling, then waiting) I got the approach about 7 s after setup and the blow at 17 s, on both phone and desktop.
- Layout: no console errors and no page scroll at 390x844 or 1280x800. No emoji.

**Largest gap: refusal leaves almost no lasting cost.** After 24 calls and a 112 cm push, I ran the same setup without restarting. She came out of hiding and approached about 8 s after setup, against about 7 s from a fresh start, and she blew the same way. Nothing in her later behaviour shows that she remembers being pushed.

**Secondary gaps**
- The end card opens by itself about 2 s after the blow (proof 21.5 s, phone about 26 s). It replaces the device with "state watch.look" and bars for refuse, hide and approach while she is still in the scene. That puts state labels on screen.
- The blow is carried by the flames, not by her body. Across proof 18–19 s her pose barely changes.
- A cake "held up" or "toward her" floats in the air with no one holding it, and at standing height it sits on the horizon line, far from the table (proof 3 s).

**Repair condition (falsifiable):** Use seed 7 and the same setup sequence. If the player calls 6 of 6 for 6 s and pushes the cake to 112 cm first, the time from setup to approach must be at least 2x the fresh time. Frames must also show a difference after the push, for example she stops short of the table or glances back at Ana before she blows.

**Preserve:** her turned-back sulk; the crouch for hiding; quick recovery when the room changes; the breath sound timed to the flames; the device's plain labels.
