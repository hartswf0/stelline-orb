THE PROOF (25 s, sound)

The cake pushed at her: she backs away, then turns her back. Ana calls: she stays turned, then sits and hides. Ana dims the room, brings the cake back slowly and lowers it, brings the three children in to sing, kneels. She peeks, looks, stands, comes, and blows on the last "you". A long HOLD at 23 s ends the memory; the end card opens once it is dark.

AFTER BEING PRESSED (48 s, the critic's condition)

Ana calls at full strength for 6 s and pushes the cake at her, then sets the same good room as the proof. She hides much longer, comes out at 20.7 s, then watches warily: she looks back at Ana and edges toward the cake and back every few seconds, arms drawn in. On her way stops short of the table with her arms half crossed, looks back at Ana for 3 s, then comes on slowly and blows at 42 s.

PHONE PLAYTEST (scripted, 390 x 844, touch)

An impatient player: calls, then sets the right circumstances, then calls once more while she is coming. She stops and turns away, and comes again later.
