THE GAME

The birthday is cut into flat pieces, each at its own depth. Kneel in the right place, and set the pieces at the right depths, so that when you release HOLD and she blows, all seven candles and all three faces are seen. If something is hidden when she blows, the memory holds again and you try once more.

THE DEVICE

SCALE RINGWhere you kneel, in degrees around the cake. Every piece slides by an amount set by its depth: near pieces fast, far pieces slow.
SELECTThe piece you work on: near child, child left, child right, balloon.
KNURLED RINGThe selected piece's depth, in steps of 5 cm. Moved back, a piece is smaller and slides less.
TRIANGLE RINGFor a child, her time (earlier or later in her performance). For the balloon, its height.
HOLDTap: freeze the children, or release them so she blows. Hold it down: switch the memory off.
LEFT COLLARUpper button plays the scene (a solved game). Lower button clears to the start.

Turn a ring by dragging up or down on it, or scroll over it. The hand turns quietly, a click a detent; the machine then steps to it at 11.8 a second, a louder click a step. The picture glides between steps.
