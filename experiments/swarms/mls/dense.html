THE BLOW · A BIRTHDAY MADE BY BENDING ONE PICTURE

Goal: shape one breath so all seven candles go out. You have three breaths. The breath's strength is read off the deformed face (how much the cheek discs grow, how large the mouth opening is). She leans, so an uncorrected breath goes 8° to one side: aim it. A flame goes out only when the breath stretches it thinner than two thirds of its width, measured on the flame's own deformed mesh. So the family you choose for the flames decides whether they can go out: affine flames thin and tear; rigid flames cannot thin and never go out.

SELECTShort press: the next thing to work: STREAMER, CHEEKS, LIPS, FLAMES. Long press (0.6 s, real time): the next deformation family (AFFINE, SIMILARITY, RIGID) for that thing's surface.
KNURLED RINGThe value of the selected thing: how far the streamer wraps the cake, how much breath the cheeks hold, how tightly the lips purse, how many candles are lit. Turn it by hand (quiet detents); the machine then steps to it at 11.8 a second, one click a step, one step at a time across all values.
TRIANGLE RINGCheeks or lips: the aim of the breath, left or right (she leans: about two detents corrects it). Streamer: its height on the cake. Flames: their size (a bigger flame needs a stronger breath).
SCALE RINGWhere you kneel: the flat layers slide against each other (the face plate behind, the cake in front).
HOLDShort press: blow. The cheeks empty under the breath, the machine stepping them down. Hold it down to switch the memory off.
