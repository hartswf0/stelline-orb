THE ARCHIVE CAKE · OPERATOR PANEL

Build the birthday cake from pieces drawn out of a museum archive, stage by stage, on the memory maker's device. SELECT picks the slot (stand, plate, mould, decoration, inscription, candles, light). The knurled ring draws a piece from the archive for that slot; the triangle ring sets its size and turn; HOLD places it, and the making plays out. Under the stand, the plate; the mould is filled and baked and lifted; the icing goes on and the beads are piped; the knurl writes 生日快乐; the candles are set; HOLD strikes the match; HOLD again and the candles go out. Long HOLD switches the memory off. Each placed decoration is scored against the film cake named at the top (MATCH); place another decoration to move toward it.

Rights, plainly: none of the museum files could be downloaded from this build's sandbox (the proxy refuses every museum host) and no record's CC0 status could be read. Every piece on stage is an original stand-in drawn in code; the record it stands for is kept with its rights state. Nothing from an archive is in the repo.
