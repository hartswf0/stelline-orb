CAKE METAMORPHOSIS · HOW TO PLAY

The cake is one surface that changes shape. Every stage of the making, and each of the film's cakes, is the same surface: the same points, moved. Carry it from nothing to dough, through the stages you choose, to the brown dome the film writes on; then the candles, the inscription, the match, and the blow.

SELECTThe next form or job: the stages and cakes this one can become, then CANDLES, INSCRIPTION, MATCH.
KNURLED RINGCarries the change forward or back, one step at a time. The machine follows your hand at 11.8 steps a second.
TRIANGLE RINGThe pacing of the next change: EVEN, EASE, LINGER (holds each shape longest), LEAN (changes at once). Set it before turning.
SCALE RINGWhere you kneel, in degrees around the cake.
HOLDSets a finished change. With the seven candles lit and the inscription written, HOLD is the blow. Hold it down to switch the memory off.
SCENE / CLEARPlays one path through the whole making; clears it.
