THE SETTLING CAKE · HOW TO PLAY

Make the film's cake (chocolate, orange top, white beads, 生日快乐, seven candles) on the stand, light it, and let her blow it out. Every material answers the stand's motion with momentum and then comes to rest. A fast hand has consequences.

SELECTThe next material: LAYER, POUR, BEADS, WRITE, CANDLES, LIGHT.
KNURLED RINGThe selected material's value: layer offset (4 mm a detent), icing amount (20 ml a detent), beads to pipe, the inscription (stroke by stroke), candle slot, candles lit. The hand turns it with quiet clicks; the machine then steps to it at 11.8 a second.
TRIANGLE RINGTilt of the stand toward her (2 degrees a detent, smooth, not stepped). Turn it slowly and the cake answers and settles. Flick it and candles lose their grip and fall; wet icing runs.
SCALE RINGTurns the stand under her eye (5 degrees a detent). The streamers and the paper crown swing and settle.
HOLDCommit: lower the layer, pour, pipe, push the candle in. When the cake matches the film's and all seven are lit and still, HOLD is the blow. Hold it down to switch the memory off.
LEFT COLLARTop: play the scene. Middle: SPLIT, the same hand on three damping presets side by side (LOOSE, TUNED, RIGID). Bottom: clear.
MATCHScore against the film's cake: three aligned layers, icing coverage without run-off, height to width near 0.40 (traced from the film), 24 beads, the inscription, seven upright candles, level, everything at rest. 90 and seven upright candles to pass.
