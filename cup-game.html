TURN AND CATCH

Motes fall toward a numbered column of the ring. Turn the cup so the gold catcher is under the mote when it lands: the catcher sits at whichever column of the cup faces the camera. Three misses end the game.

Prop the phone up with its screen toward you, hold the cup about 25 cm in front of it, and show the camera the ruler strip once to begin.
