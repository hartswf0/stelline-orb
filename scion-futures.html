2 · Six Non-Negotiable SCION Invariants

Hard lessons verified across 5 Blind Critic rounds & 217 video captures
I · CONTROLLER LOCK & `y_split` DE-CHROMING

Generative video models redraw complex UI panels over time. Either attach the ?museum=1 screenless 5-ring barrel as a UI-lock reference, OR generate LAY-03 clean stage-only video and composite the real WebGL controller beneath via tools/flow/stack.sh. Always strip y >= y_split before CV segmentation.

II · SIDE `)` CRESCENT RETICLE ONLY

Never wrap numbered lists, HUD callouts, or concentric circles around the cake. Use either reticle=off (zero reticle) or the single pale smoked-glass ) crescent standing to the right of the cake (reticle=aside), curving concave-toward-cake.

III · 100% ORB-FREE WORLD PLATES

Inside the holographic memory room, never show a handheld orb, lens, phone, or glowing gadget in any character's hands (only a cake knife, match, or plate). The Orb belongs strictly to the operator's hands below.

IV · PER-LAYER INDEPENDENT CLOCKS (`r_i`)

Every addressable layer (cake, icing, flames, children, beetle_eyes) runs its own local clock q_i(t) = q_i(t_0) + r_i(t - t_0) + dq_i. Holding children at r=0 while rotating the lit cake (r=1) sweeps live 3200K bounce across frozen faces.

V · TRANSIENT MECHANICAL FOLEY ONLY

Zero continuous OscillatorNode drones or synth pads. Emit only rate-limited (>55ms), 3–5ms filtered noise micro-clicks (1100–3100Hz) on physical ball-detent crossings and switch actuations.

VI · PRACTICAL AUTHORING & DUAL CRITICS

Every speculative instrument must let anyone create something real (custom piped birthday cakes, custom insect holotypes, .USDA/.GLB/.png exports) and pass both Blind Visual Critic A and Operative Critic B.
