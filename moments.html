STELLINE · THE MOMENTS

In these moments her hands on the orb, the change on screen and the white reticle can be read together. Each was pulled at 12 frames a second and measured: the sharpness of each of the beetle's eyes, the cake's size and tilt, the reticle's needles, how much of each child is there, and the light on a face. The measurements are curves, and the orb plays them: in these beats ring 4 is turned by the film itself, and turning it by hand finds the frame where the film had that value.

OBSERVED means it is in the frames. INFERRED means it is a reading of what the orb did, made from what is observed.
