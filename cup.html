CUP ORB

Your wrapped cup becomes a controller. The camera reads which cell you touch, and a twin of the cup turns on screen.

  1. Allow the camera when the browser asks.
  2. Hold the cup about 25 cm from the phone, upright or sideways, ruler strip toward the camera.
  3. Touch four cells as they are named. That calibrates it to your hand and your camera.

The picture stays on this phone. Nothing is uploaded.
