One hundred memories

One for every age from 1 to 100. Each was made on Field Orb 06 by turning its rings through that person's guide, keeping a frame after every step, and then checked against its plan: the pieces of clay, the name written to the end, a lit candle for every year (twelve at most), three children arriving, every candle out in the last frame.

The picture is a film frame of the moment before the blow: lit, with its guests, when the whole memory holds solid. The guests are people cut from the archive's own birthday clips, and the room is another birthday of the same kind. Open one to watch it made on the orb, with the mist and the timeline.
